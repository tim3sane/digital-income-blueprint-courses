Privacy Policy

Last updated:

In short: Vocari has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies. What you type, the photos you add and the phrases you speak stay on your device and never reach us. Only if you buy a membership do we process the minimum data needed to bill you, through Stripe. We do not sell or share data for advertising.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller is Vocari, a project based in Chile ("Vocari", "we"). For any question or to exercise your rights, email . We are not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer; this address serves as our privacy contact.

2. Laws we follow

Vocari is available worldwide. This policy is designed to comply, as applicable to each user, with Chile's Law 19,628 on the Protection of Private Life and Law 21,719 replacing it once in force; the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR; Brazil's LGPD; the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA); and the data protection laws of Argentina, Colombia, Mexico, Peru and other countries. If your local law gives you more rights, those apply.

3. Data that stays on your device (we never receive it)

  • Profiles, boards, buttons, photos, saved phrases, recent history and usage statistics.
  • Settings: language, voice, speed, size, theme and lock.

It is stored in your browser's local storage. Because this content may reveal sensitive information (for example about health or disability), we chose never to receive it. Speech is generated by your operating system's text-to-speech engine (Google, Apple, Microsoft or other), governed by their own policies. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, this information is lost: use "Export all" to keep a backup.

5. Service providers and international transfers

We do not sell, rent or "share" (as defined by the CCPA) personal information. Only these providers are involved:

  • Stripe, Inc. (USA/Ireland): payment processing and subscription portal. For its own purposes such as fraud prevention, Stripe acts as an independent controller. Stripe's policy.
  • Vercel Inc. (USA): website and payment function hosting. Vercel's policy.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the USA, protected by safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We may disclose data if legally required by a competent authority.

6. Retention

  • Billing data: while you have a subscription and afterwards for the period required by Chilean tax law (generally 6 years).
  • Support and scholarship emails: up to 24 months.
  • Server logs: the short period set by our hosting provider.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, objection, portability, restriction and not to be subject to automated decisions. California residents have the right to know, correct and delete their personal information and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights; since we do not sell or share personal information, there is nothing to opt out of. Email from the address you paid with. We reply within 30 days, or sooner if your law requires. Exercising your rights is free.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority (for example your EU/UK supervisory authority, Brazil's ANPD, or Chile's Personal Data Protection Agency once operational).

8. Children

Vocari may be used by children and people with disabilities under adult supervision. Because we create no accounts and never receive app content, we do not collect personal information from children (COPPA, GDPR art. 8 and equivalent laws). Only adults with legal capacity (parents, guardians, therapists or institutions) may buy memberships. If you believe a child sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) and cryptographically signed membership codes, and we never store card data. No system is 100% secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify you and the authorities within legal deadlines.

10. Cookies and local storage

Vocari uses no tracking or advertising cookies, only the browser's local storage, which is strictly necessary for the app to work. During checkout, Stripe may use its own cookies to prevent fraud.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If the change is material and you have a membership, we will email you before it takes effect.
