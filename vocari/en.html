FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and use it. If you buy a membership, you get a restore code to activate it on other devices.

How do I install it on my phone?

Open Vocari in Chrome (Android) and tap "Install app" or "Add to home screen". On iPhone/iPad, open it in Safari and tap Share → "Add to Home Screen". It then works offline.

The voice sounds odd or doesn't play

Vocari uses your device's built-in voice. Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text-to-speech. iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices. Download a good-quality voice and choose it in Vocari's settings.

Where are my photos and boards stored?

Only on your device. We never see them or upload them. That's why we recommend using "Export all" now and then as a backup.

What happens if I cancel?

Nothing you created is deleted. Your photos, boards and profiles keep working; you just can't create new paid items.

Can I use it in a medical emergency?

Vocari helps people communicate, but it is not a medical device or an emergency system. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services. Read the health notice.

Does it replace a therapist?

No. It's a tool. Ideally use it with a speech-language therapist, who can help choose and teach vocabulary.
