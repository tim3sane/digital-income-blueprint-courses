Refund Policy

Last updated:

In short: try it free for 7 days. If you were charged and it didn't work for you, we refund 100% if you ask within 14 days of any charge, wherever you live.

1. Free trial

Plus and Professional memberships include 7 days free. If you cancel during the trial, you are not charged.

2. 14-day guarantee (includes your right of withdrawal)

If you pay for a membership (monthly, yearly or a renewal) or the Vocari Guide, and aren't satisfied, email us within 14 calendar days of the charge and we will refund 100% of it, no questions asked. This meets and exceeds the 10-day withdrawal right under Chile's Law 19,496 (art. 3 bis) and the EU's 14-day right of withdrawal. You don't lose it by having started to use paid features.

3. After 14 days

  • Monthly plans: cancel anytime so they don't renew. We don't give partial refunds for the current month.
  • Yearly plans: we refund the pro-rata share of the full months you haven't used.

4. Charges made in error

If you were charged twice, if a subscription you had already canceled renewed, or if we made any other mistake, we refund the full amount regardless of timing.

5. How to request a refund

Email from the address you paid with, stating the charge date. You can also request it directly from Paddle via your receipt link or at paddle.net. We process refunds within 5 business days, to the same payment method. Your bank may take another 5–10 business days to show it. Refunds are free of charge.

6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not reduce your rights under the law of your country. If the law grants you a broader right, that right applies.
